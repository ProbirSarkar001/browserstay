"Merging PDF" is one of the most searched PDF tasks online. The results page is crowded — iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Adobe, PDF24, and dozens of clones.

They all promise to combine your files for free. The differences are in privacy, limits, and what happens to your documents after you click merge.

## What We Compared

- **Processing** — server upload vs browser-local
- **Free tier limits** — daily tasks, file size caps, page limits
- **Watermarks** on output
- **Account requirements**
- **Privacy** — where your files go

## Server-Upload Mergers

**iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Adobe, PDF24, Sejda** dominate search results. They're polished and familiar.

**Strengths:**
- Drag-and-drop interfaces refined over years
- Handle very large files on powerful servers
- Additional tools (compress, convert, sign) on the same platform
- Mobile-friendly

**Weaknesses:**
- **Files upload before merging** — your documents sit on their infrastructure
- **Smallpdf: 2 free merges per day** — easy to hit the wall
- **Sejda: 50MB file size limit** on free tier
- **Adobe: account required** — another login to manage
- Privacy depends on trusting their deletion policies

**Use when:** Merging public documents, brochures, or anything you'd post on social media.

## Browser-Based: BrowserStay

Merges PDFs in your browser without uploading.

**Strengths:**
- No upload — files stay on your PC
- No daily limits or file size caps (within browser memory)
- No watermarks, no account, open source
- Reorder files by drag-and-drop before merging

**Weaknesses:**
- No OCR, e-sign, or advanced editing
- Initial page load downloads processing code
- Very large merges slower on low-end devices

**Use when:** Combining invoices, contracts, tax documents, or client files.

## Desktop: PDFsam & Mac Preview

**PDFsam Basic** (free, cross-platform) and **Mac Preview** merge offline.

**Strengths:** True offline privacy, unlimited use, no browser needed
**Weaknesses:** Install required; Preview Mac-only; PDFsam UI is utilitarian

**Use when:** You merge PDFs regularly and want a permanent local solution.

## Common Complaints (and Solutions)

### "I hit the daily limit on Smallpdf"

Free server tools ration usage to push subscriptions. BrowserStay and PDFsam have no daily caps.

### "There's a watermark on my merged PDF"

Some tools watermark output on free tiers. BrowserStay, iLovePDF merge, and PDF24 don't watermark merges — but always check before processing important files.

### "I don't want to create another account"

Adobe online requires login. BrowserStay, PDF24, Sejda, and iLovePDF work without accounts (with varying limits).

### "I'm merging confidential client documents"

Don't upload. Use BrowserStay (browser) or PDFsam/Preview (desktop). Architecture beats interface for sensitive work.
