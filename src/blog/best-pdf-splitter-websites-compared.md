You need to extract a few pages from a PDF. Google returns dozens of "split PDF free" options. They all look the same — drag, drop, download.

They're not the same. Some upload your file to a server. Some add watermarks. Some cap how many pages you can split per day. Here's how the most popular options compare.

## What We Compared

For each tool, we looked at:

- **Processing** — does your file upload to a server, or stay on your device?
- **Cost** — truly free, or free with limits?
- **Watermarks** — any branding on the output?
- **Account required** — sign-up needed?
- **Page limits** — caps on file size or page count?
- **Privacy** — what happens to your file after processing?

## Server-Upload Tools: iLovePDF, Smallpdf, Adobe, PDF24, Sejda

These are the names you'll see most often in search results. They work well for non-sensitive files:

**Pros:**
- Mature, polished interfaces
- Fast processing on powerful servers
- Extra features (OCR, compression, e-sign) on some platforms
- Work on any device with a browser

**Cons:**
- **Your file is uploaded** — it travels to their server before splitting
- **Free tiers have limits** — Smallpdf allows 2 tasks per day; Sejda caps at 3 per hour and 200 pages
- **Privacy policies vary** — most claim temporary storage, but you can't verify deletion
- **Adobe requires an account** for online tools

**Best for:** Public documents, non-sensitive files, when you need extra features like OCR and don't mind uploading.

## Browser-Based Local Tools: BrowserStay

BrowserStay splits PDFs entirely in your browser. No upload step.

**Pros:**
- **Files never leave your device** — no server sees your document
- **No daily limits** — split as many PDFs as your device can handle
- **No watermarks, no account**
- **Open source** — the code is public; claims are verifiable
- Works on Mac, Windows, Linux, Chromebooks

**Cons:**
- Very large PDFs (100+ MB) may be slower on older devices
- Fewer advanced features than full desktop suites (no OCR, no e-sign)
- Requires an internet connection to load the page initially

**Best for:** Contracts, financial documents, medical records, anything you wouldn't email to a stranger.

## Desktop Tools: PDFsam, Mac Preview

**PDFsam** (Windows, Mac, Linux) and **Mac Preview** split PDFs offline with no upload at all.

**Pros:** Maximum privacy, no internet needed after install, no limits
**Cons:** Installation required; Preview is Mac-only; PDFsam's advanced features are paid

**Best for:** Users who split PDFs frequently and prefer installed software.

## How to Choose

| Your situation | Best choice |
|----------------|-------------|
| Sensitive document (contract, tax, medical) | BrowserStay or desktop tool |
| Public file, quick one-off split | Any server-upload tool works |
| Hit daily limits on Smallpdf/Sejda | BrowserStay or PDFsam |
| Need OCR or e-sign | Adobe or iLovePDF (accept upload tradeoff) |
| Mac user, simple split | Preview (built-in) |
| Want to verify privacy claims | BrowserStay (open source) |

## The Privacy Question

Splitting a PDF seems harmless — you're just extracting pages. But the file you split might be a employment contract, a medical report, or a client's financial statement.

Server-upload tools work fine when the document doesn't matter. When it does, the architecture matters more than the interface.
