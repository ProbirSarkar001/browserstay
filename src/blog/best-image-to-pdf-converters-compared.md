You scanned five pages of a form. You have a folder of product photos for a catalog. You photographed receipts for your accountant. They all need to become one PDF.

Image-to-PDF is a common task — and like most PDF tools, the popular options upload your images first.

## What We Compared

- **Processing** — server upload vs browser-local
- **Input formats** — JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC
- **Page ordering** — drag-and-drop reorder
- **Page size settings** — A4, Letter, fit to image
- **Batch support**
- **Free limits**
- **Privacy**

## Server-Upload Converters

**iLovePDF, Smallpdf, jpg2pdf.com, Adobe, PDF24** all require uploading your images.

**Strengths:**
- Mature interfaces with page preview
- Adobe and PDF24 offer fine page size control
- jpg2pdf.com is simple and unlimited for JPG
- Handle large batches on server hardware

**Weaknesses:**
- **Images uploaded to third-party servers** — scanned IDs, receipts, personal photos
- **Smallpdf: 2 tasks/day**
- **jpg2pdf.com: JPG only**, no reordering
- Privacy depends on deletion policies

**Best for:** Non-sensitive images, marketing materials, public documents.

## Browser-Based: BrowserStay

Combines images into a PDF locally with drag-and-drop page ordering.

**Strengths:**
- Images never leave your device
- JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC input
- Reorder pages before generating
- Page size and margin settings
- No limits, no account, open source

**Weaknesses:**
- No TIFF input
- Very large batches may take time on older devices

**Best for:** Scanned documents, receipt photos, client images, anything sensitive.

## Desktop: Mac Preview

Mac users can select images in Preview and export as PDF.

**Strengths:** Built-in, offline, unlimited
**Weaknesses:** Mac only, less intuitive for batch reordering

**Best for:** Mac users with a few images already open in Preview.

## Tips for Better PDFs

1. **Reorder before generating** — fix page sequence now, not after
2. **Consistent orientation** — rotate images before converting if needed
3. **Choose page size** — A4 or Letter for documents; fit-to-image for photos
4. **Compress after** if the PDF is too large — convert heavy pages to images, compress, rebuild

## How to Choose

| Situation | Best choice |

| Product catalog images | BrowserStay or PDF24 |
| Quick JPG-only, public images | jpg2pdf.com |
| Mac user, few images | Mac Preview |
| Need TIFF input | Adobe or PDF24 (accept upload) |
