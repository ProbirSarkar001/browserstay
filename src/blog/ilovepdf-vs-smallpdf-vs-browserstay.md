## iLovePDF

**What it is:** A comprehensive PDF platform with merge, split, compress, convert, OCR, e-sign, and more. Based in Europe.

**Strengths:**
- Huge feature set — if you need OCR or e-signatures, iLovePDF has them
- Polished interface, mobile apps for iOS and Android
- Generous enough free tier for occasional use
- No watermarks on merge/split

**Weaknesses:**
- Files upload to their servers before processing
- Free tier has daily task limits
- Closed source — you trust their privacy policy
- Premium subscription for heavy users ($7+/month)

**Best for:** Users who need OCR, e-sign, or PDF editing and accept the upload tradeoff.

## Smallpdf

**What it is:** Another full-featured PDF platform, similar scope to iLovePDF. Swiss-based, strong brand recognition.

**Strengths:**
- Clean, modern interface
- Desktop apps for Mac and Windows
- Good integration with Google Drive and Dropbox
- Strong mobile apps

**Weaknesses:**
- **Only 2 free tasks per day** — the most restrictive major platform
- Files upload to servers
- Premium required for regular use ($9+/month)
- Closed source

**Best for:** Occasional users who need one or two PDF tasks per day and don't mind uploading.

## BrowserStay

**What it is:** A privacy-first collection of PDF and image tools that run entirely in your browser. Open source.

**Strengths:**
- **Files never uploaded** — processing happens on your device
- **No daily limits** — use as much as your device can handle
- **No account, no watermarks, no paywall**
- **Open source** — verify privacy claims in the code
- Merge, split, encrypt, unlock, PDF↔image conversion

**Weaknesses:**
- No OCR, e-sign, or advanced PDF editing
- No native mobile apps (works in mobile browsers)
- Very large files slower on older devices
- Fewer features than the upload giants

**Best for:** Privacy-conscious users, sensitive documents, daily PDF tasks without hitting limits.

## The Privacy Difference

This is the fundamental architectural split:

**iLovePDF and Smallpdf:** Your file → their server → processed → returned. They see your document. You trust their deletion policy.

**BrowserStay:** Your file → browser memory → processed locally → downloaded. They never see your document. You verify this in open-source code.

For a public brochure, the difference doesn't matter. For a tax return, employment contract, or medical record, it matters a lot.
