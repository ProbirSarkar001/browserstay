iLovePDF is one of the most popular free PDF platforms — merge, split, compress, convert, OCR, e-sign, and more. It's also a server-upload service: every file you process travels to their infrastructure before you get the result.

If you want more privacy, no daily limits, or a different feature mix, here are the best alternatives.

## Why People Switch from iLovePDF

1. **Files uploaded to servers** — privacy concern for sensitive documents
2. **Daily task limits** on free tier
3. **Premium subscription** (~$7/month) for heavy users
4. **Closed source** — can't verify privacy claims
5. **Want offline processing** — no internet dependency after setup

## 1. BrowserStay — Privacy-First Alternative

Same core PDF tasks without uploading.

**Covers:** Merge, split, encrypt, unlock, PDF to image, PDF to Markdown, image to PDF
**Advantages:** Files stay on your device, unlimited free use, open source
**Missing:** OCR, e-sign, PDF editing, compression (use image workflow)

Best for: Sensitive documents, daily PDF tasks, users who want verifiable privacy.

## 2. PDF24 — Generous Free Server Alternative

EU-based platform with a broad free toolkit.

**Covers:** Everything iLovePDF does, plus a desktop app
**Advantages:** More generous free tier, no account required, desktop app for Windows
**Missing:** Privacy (files still uploaded)

Best for: Users who want iLovePDF-like features with fewer restrictions.

## 3. PDFsam — Offline Desktop Alternative

Open-source desktop app for merge and split.

**Covers:** Merge, split (free); rotate, extract (paid)
**Advantages:** Fully offline, unlimited, open source
**Missing:** Encrypt, convert, compress, OCR

Best for: Frequent merge/split users who want permanent offline software.

## 4. Adobe Acrobat — Professional Alternative

Industry standard for PDF editing.

**Covers:** Everything — edit, OCR, e-sign, forms, accessibility
**Advantages:** Most complete feature set, trusted brand
**Missing:** Expensive ($12+/month), files uploaded for online tools

Best for: Professionals who need full PDF editing and accept the cost.

## 5. Smallpdf — Direct Competitor

Very similar to iLovePDF in scope and approach.

**Covers:** Full PDF toolkit with mobile apps
**Advantages:** Polished UI, cloud integrations
**Missing:** Only 2 free tasks/day (more restrictive than iLovePDF)

Best for: Occasional use when you need mobile apps and cloud sync.

## Feature Gap: What BrowserStay Doesn't Have (Yet)

Being honest about tradeoffs:

- **No OCR** — can't make scanned PDFs searchable
- **No e-signatures** — can't sign documents digitally
- **No PDF text editing** — can't add/remove text from existing PDFs
- **No form filling** — can't fill interactive PDF forms
- **No cloud storage** — no Google Drive/Dropbox integration

If you need these features on non-sensitive files, iLovePDF or PDF24 remain good choices. For everything else, BrowserStay covers the core tasks without uploading.
