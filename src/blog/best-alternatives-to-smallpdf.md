Smallpdf is polished, well-known, and everywhere in Google results. It's also one of the most restrictive free PDF platforms — **2 tasks per day** — and every file you process is uploaded to their servers.

If you've hit the limit, need more privacy, or want to avoid another subscription, here are the best alternatives.

## Why People Look for Alternatives

1. **2 tasks/day free limit** — easy to hit with normal use
2. **$9+/month** for Pro — adds up for basic PDF tasks
3. **Files uploaded to servers** — privacy concern for sensitive documents
4. **Account prompts** — friction for quick one-off tasks
5. **Watermarks** on some free-tier tools

## 2. iLovePDF — Best for Feature Parity

**What it does:** Everything Smallpdf does — merge, split, compress, convert, OCR, e-sign, edit.

**Why it's an alternative:**
- Similar feature set to Smallpdf
- More generous free tier (more than 2/day)
- Mobile apps available
- No watermarks on merge/split

**Tradeoff:** Still uploads files to servers. Premium ~$7/month.

**Switch if:** You need OCR or e-sign and can accept server uploads.

## 3. PDF24 — Best Free Server Alternative

**What it does:** Full PDF toolkit with generous free tier.

**Why it's an alternative:**
- Much more generous than Smallpdf's 2/day
- No account required
- EU-based servers
- Desktop app available for Windows

**Tradeoff:** Files still uploaded. Ad-supported.

**Switch if:** You need server-side processing but want more free tasks.

## 4. PDFsam — Best Desktop Alternative

**What it does:** Merge and split PDFs offline.

**Why it's an alternative:**
- Fully offline — no upload ever
- Unlimited free merge/split
- Cross-platform (Windows, Mac, Linux)
- Open source

**Tradeoff:** Install required. Advanced features paid. No encrypt/unlock on free tier.

**Switch if:** You merge/split frequently and want a permanent offline tool.

## 5. Mac Preview — Best for Mac Users

**What it does:** Built-in PDF merge and page extraction.

**Why it's an alternative:**
- Already on your Mac
- Fully offline, unlimited
- No install, no account

**Tradeoff:** Mac only. Limited to merge/split — no encrypt, convert, or compress.

**Switch if:** You're on Mac and only need basic merge/split.
